# justwhyus.ma — Project Goals

## Vision

Become the go-to digital studio for Moroccan businesses that need more than a pretty website — businesses that need a **system** that works as hard as they do.

---

## Core Mission

> "We understand your industry deeply."

justwhyus exists to close the gap between what Moroccan businesses need digitally and what generic web agencies deliver. We specialize. We go deep. We build systems that fit the actual workflow of restaurants, car rentals, travel agencies, and real estate companies — not one-size-fits-all templates.

---

## Website Goals

### Primary Goal
**Brand identity** — When someone lands on justwhyus.ma, they should immediately feel:
- This studio is serious
- They understand MY industry
- They're different from every other agency I've seen

### NOT the goal
- ❌ Lead generation (no aggressive popups or forms above the fold)
- ❌ Portfolio showcase (no case studies yet)
- ❌ Service pricing page
