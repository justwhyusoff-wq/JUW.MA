# justwhyus.ma — Project Skills

## 1. Industry Intelligence
We don't study websites. We study businesses.

Before touching a line of code for any industry project, the team maps:
- How the business actually operates day-to-day
- Where clients drop off or get frustrated digitally
- What a system needs to do vs what looks good
- Local Moroccan market specifics (language, habits, payment, trust signals)

**Covered industries**: Restaurants, Car Rentals, Travel Agencies, Real Estate, E-commerce, Professional Services.
